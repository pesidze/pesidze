02 · Performance

Every second costs you customers.

A slow page loses people before they see what you sell. They leave, they don't come back, and you keep paying for the ads that sent them.

Speed is the cheapest conversion improvement you can make, because it improves every page at once.

+32%

Increase in the probability of a visitor bouncing when page load time goes from 1 to 3 seconds.

Google / SOASTA, 2017
+90%

Increase in bounce probability when load time goes from 1 to 5 seconds.

Google / SOASTA, 2017
+8.4%

More retail conversions from a 0.1 second improvement in mobile site speed.

Deloitte, "Milliseconds Make Millions", 2020
Our launch guarantee

How fast should a website be? Faster than you can think.

Every site also ships with security headers configured.
What they are and why they matter →

Under 0.1 seconds

Feels instant. The site reacts as fast as the visitor can click, so nothing breaks their focus.

Under 1 second

Visitors notice a short pause but stay on track. This is the target for loading a page.

1 to 10 seconds

Each second of waiting makes visitors more likely to lose interest. Past 10 seconds, most will leave unless they can see the page is still loading.

Source: Jakob Nielsen, Response Times: The 3 Important Limits, Nielsen Norman Group.

03 · Sustainability

A website that cleans up after itself.

Every page view burns a little electricity, and electricity usually means carbon. Ours go the other way.

Lighter pages already use less energy, because there's less to send. Then we measure what's left and remove more CO₂ than your site puts out, through certified carbon removal, for as long as we look after it. Net result: your website takes carbon out of the air instead of adding to it.
