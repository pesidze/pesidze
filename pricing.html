Process

How a project runs.

Performance and security are set as targets on day one and checked at every stage, not patched in after launch.

  1. 01

    Audit

    We measure your current site's speed, security headers and conversion data.

  2. 02

    Design

    Interface and content designed within an agreed performance budget.

  3. 03

    Build

    Frontend and backend developed together, tested on real mid-range phones.

  4. 04

    Launch

    Hosting, caching, TLS and security headers configured and verified.

  5. 05

    Support

    Uptime, speed and security monitored, with updates handled by us.

What affects the price

Why no two quotes are the same.

These are the things we look at when we put your quote together.

Size

How many pages and page types the site has. A five-page site and a fifty-page site are different jobs.

Functionality

Online shop, bookings, member areas, forms, search or anything custom that goes beyond what WordPress does out of the box.

Languages

Each extra language means more pages to set up, check and keep working.

Integrations

Connecting your CRM, payment provider, newsletter, booking or stock system.

Content

Whether you bring your own text and images, or need copywriting, photo selection or content moved over from an old site.

Branding

Working with your existing brand, or creating a visual identity from scratch first.

Timeline

Rush deadlines mean rearranging other work, so faster delivery can cost more.

Custom build

When WordPress isn't the right fit, we build a custom site. More flexibility, more development time.

Third-party costs

Hosting, domain, premium plugins and licences are billed at cost and listed separately in your quote.
